Booking Confirmed

You're booked in

Payment's gone through and your session's confirmed. A confirmation email is on its way to your inbox now.

What happens next

About 20 minutes before your slot, we'll email you again, this one tells you exactly which seat (or seats) are ready for your group.

When that email lands, head straight to the seat number it gives you and scan the QR code there to start your session, no need to queue at the door first.

Running early, or that email hasn't shown up yet? Just say your name at the counter and a member of staff will sort you out.

Before you visit

Check your inbox (and spam folder, just in case) for both emails, your booking confirmation now, and your seat assignment closer to the time.

Got a question about your booking? Get in touch at info@digznlidz.co.uk.
